## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Selection follows defined criteria
The selection SHALL evaluate recipes against three criteria: poetic craftsmanship, emotional resonance, and template versatility.

#### Scenario: Criteria applied
- **WHEN** selecting the three best recipes from the book
- **THEN** each chosen recipe SHALL be accompanied by a brief rationale explaining how it meets the selection criteria

### Requirement: Each recipe is fully presented
The article SHALL present each selected recipe with its name, rhyme scheme, structural template, an example composition, and usage guidance.

#### Scenario: Recipe structure complete
- **WHEN** a recipe is presented in the article
- **THEN** it SHALL include all of: rhyme scheme notation, structural template, a composed example, and practical tips for adaptation

### Requirement: Selection rationale is documented
The article SHALL explain why each of the three recipes was chosen over the alternatives.

#### Scenario: Rationale provided
- **WHEN** presenting a selected recipe
- **THEN** the article SHALL include a paragraph explaining the recipe's strengths and why it merits inclusion in the top three
