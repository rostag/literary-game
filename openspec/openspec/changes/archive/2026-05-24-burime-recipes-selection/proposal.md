## Why

The book "1000+1 Burime for Those Who Have Known Life" contains a wealth of burime recipes — poetic templates and formulas — but lacks a curated highlight selection. A focused presentation of the three best recipes will showcase the book's value and serve as a standalone reference for poetry enthusiasts.

## What Changes

- Review and analyze all 1000+1 burime recipes from the book
- Select the three highest-quality, most impactful recipes based on poetic merit, emotional resonance, and versatility
- Present each recipe with its structure, example composition, and usage guidance
- Publish as a new markdown document
